# Final manuscript results

32 TCGA cohorts; 10,433 participant-unique tumors; 500 loci selected by observed mutation counts per cohort; all 124,750 pairs per cohort, including same-gene M:N pairs.

MutSigCV2 is the primary background. One profile likelihood-ratio test per pair uses the final chi-square reference with 3 degrees of freedom and within-cohort Benjamini–Yekutieli q ≤ 0.05. Direction follows rho. There are 8 ME and 4 CO calls. No model fitting or simulations were performed during export.

Fisher, DISCOVER, MEGSA and WeSME/WeSCO native scores come from the completed final 500-locus analyses. BH q values for comparison methods are calculated over the full within-cohort family. MEGSA uses p < 0.001. Ranked examples can be explored independently of significance.

The source bindings, checks against final Table S5 and the BRCA/UCEC networks and LGG/UCEC biological examples are recorded in verification.json. Shared physical SNV counts are retained in the model table. Such overlap and same-gene pairs need biological interpretation.

CBaSE/DIG analyses from earlier contracts remain available through the historical release selector, and are not mixed into this final-results release.
